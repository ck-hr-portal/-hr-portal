Data Backup

Dev and Darleendy take a full copy of the portal data every week and keep it on the company server. This page shows when the last one was done.

1. Take a backup now

The backup contains salaries, loans, IDs and personal details. A password is strongly recommended. Keep the password safe: without it the file cannot be opened, and nobody can recover it for you.

2. Check or unlock a backup file

Pick a saved backup to confirm it opens and see what is inside. For a password-protected file, type its password first. After checking, you can save an unlocked copy, which is what the Firebase console needs for a restore.

What is and is not in the backup

  • Included: employees, leave and leave adjustments, salary, loans and repayments, assets, flight allowance, leavers, document history, holidays, announcements, policies, audit log, leave requests, CEO message, org chart, pending hires, final settlements, staff login links and compensatory leave.
  • Not included: the staff login accounts themselves (emails and passwords are held by Firebase Authentication), and the staff "safe copies", which the portal rebuilds on its own.
  • Weekly: the dashboard shows a reminder when the last backup is 7 or more days old.
  • Restore: Firebase console, Realtime Database, the three-dot menu, Import JSON. Practise on a spare test project first, never straight onto the live data.

🔑 Employee Portal Access

Give staff their own login to the HR portal. They sign in with their company email and choose their own password from an emailed link — nobody in HR ever sees or sets a staff password. Visible only to Dev and Darleendy.

How it works
1. The employee needs a Company Email in Employee Records.
2. Click Create login & email setup link. They receive an email and choose their own password.
3. If they forget it later, they tap Forgot password? on the login page (or you click Resend password email).
4. Access switches off by itself when an employee is marked Inactive, is moved to Leavers, or their exit date passes. You can also turn it off yourself at any time.
